# Coffee Shop Sales Analysis (Power BI)

**Author:** Doan Duy Minh Nguyen
**Course:** [ISOM 845], Suffolk University (Sawyer Business School)
**Tools:** Power BI

## Project Overview

This project analyzes sales data from a coffee shop to find out when, where, and what drives revenue. I built a 5-page interactive Power BI report so a business owner can track monthly performance, spot the busiest hours, see which products matter most, and plan for the months ahead.

## Business Problem

A coffee shop wants to grow revenue and run more efficiently. The owner needs to know:

- Is the business growing, and how fast?
- Which hours of the day bring in the most sales?
- Which products drive revenue, and which ones are not worth the inventory?
- Does performance depend on the store location?

## Report Pages

1. **Monthly Trend:** KPI cards, a monthly sales trend, and a month-by-month comparison
2. **Hour Analysis:** sales by hour of the day, with slicers to filter the view
3. **Product Analysis:** revenue by product category, a treemap, and a scatter plot of product performance
4. **Forecasting:** a projection of future sales based on the trend
5. **Best Selling Product by Month:** a decomposition tree to explore what drives revenue

## Key Findings

- Sales rise steadily from January to June, and revenue climbs sharply after February.
- Morning hours (8 to 10 AM) bring in the most revenue, while afternoons are weaker.
- Coffee and tea are the main revenue drivers.
- A small number of products generate most of the revenue, confirmed by the decomposition tree.
- Several products earn very little, which points to inefficient inventory.
- Performance is fairly balanced across store locations, so growth does not depend on one location.

## Recommendations

1. **Improve the afternoon:** use promotions or targeted offers to lift slow afternoon sales.
2. **Focus on top products:** make sure best sellers are always in stock and promoted.
3. **Review low-revenue products:** reduce or drop items that sell poorly to cut inventory waste.
4. **Staff for the morning rush:** schedule enough staff and supplies for 8 to 10 AM.
